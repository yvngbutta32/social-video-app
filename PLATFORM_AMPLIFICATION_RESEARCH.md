# Official platform amplification research

**Date:** 2026-08-25

## TikTok

TikTok’s official Content Posting API documentation states that applications can let creators post directly to their TikTok profile or upload content as a draft for later editing and posting. Direct Post supports creator-selected posting settings such as captions, hashtags, and audience visibility. The documentation describes reducing the steps for creators to share content, not forcing recommendation-feed placement or guaranteeing reach.

Source: [TikTok Content Posting API](https://developers.tiktok.com/products/content-posting-api/)

## Instagram

Meta’s official Instagram Content Publishing documentation describes publishing images, videos, Reels, and carousels for Instagram professional accounts. The requirements vary by login path and include creator/business permissions and access tokens. The documentation also identifies operational constraints such as public media hosting for publishing attempts, Page Publishing Authorization in some configurations, and a publishing-rate limit. The API provides publishing capability and status tracking; it does not provide control over ranking or guaranteed audience distribution.

Source: [Meta Instagram Content Publishing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/content-publishing)

## Product implication

ViralBoost should implement amplification as a creator-authorized distribution system: prepare platform-native assets, schedule or publish through official capabilities where approved, track delivery and performance, and optimize the next experiment. It must not use fake engagement, unauthorized access, spam, bot networks, or claims of guaranteed impressions or timeline placement. Reach-plan UI and APIs should distinguish planned distribution from confirmed delivery and observed performance.
