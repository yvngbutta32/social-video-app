# ViralBoost Product-Need Research

## Evidence collected

IAB’s 2025 Creator Economy Ad Spend & Strategy Report states that U.S. creator ad spend was projected to reach $37 billion in 2025, up 26% year over year, and identifies measurement, standards, and operational tools as major improvement opportunities. It also reports that 48% of creator ad buyers consider creators a must-buy channel and that three in four brands use or plan to use AI for creator-marketing tasks.

A peer-reviewed CHI 2024 study of short-form video creators used interviews with 17 creators and a participatory design workshop with 12 creators. Its abstract identifies creator needs around inspiration, fast access to video sources, and automated highlight generation, while warning that creator identity, creative control, and information-bubble risks must be preserved.

An LSE longitudinal ethnographic study of influencer industries found that content creators experience stress and burnout, with smaller creators particularly exposed to visibility uncertainty and algorithmic discrimination. The study frames cross-platform work as precarious labor rather than a reliably meritocratic path.

## Product implication

There is a genuine need for a system that reduces the operational burden of cross-platform creator work: importing owned media, generating platform-native adaptations, retaining creator control, publishing through authorized integrations, and connecting creative changes to measurable outcomes. The need is strongest for invite-only creator teams, managers, and high-output creators who need a private operating system rather than another standalone editor or scheduler.

The category is competitive. CapCut addresses fast mobile editing and captions. Descript addresses transcript-oriented editing. OpusClip and similar products address long-form-to-short-form repurposing. Hootsuite and Sprout Social address multi-channel publishing, analytics, and social intelligence. ViralBoost therefore needs a narrower differentiated wedge: a private creator operating system that combines source intake, creator-owned adaptation briefs, non-destructive editing, action-readiness gates, private review, authorized publishing boundaries, and evidence-backed experiment measurement in one workspace.

## Claims boundary

No product can guarantee views, virality, recommendation placement, follower growth, or public-feed distribution on third-party platforms. ViralBoost can improve the probability and efficiency of distribution through content quality, platform-native adaptation, authorized publishing, first-party discovery, controlled experiments, and measurement. Any product language should avoid guaranteed-reach claims.
